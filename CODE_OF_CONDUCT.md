# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, role, or level of
technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Share context, acknowledge uncertainty, and avoid overstating evidence.
- Respect privacy, security embargoes, licenses, and attribution.
- Help keep discussions focused and accessible to new contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, unwanted sexual attention,
insults, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publication of private information,
bad-faith disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable. Repeatedly ignoring
maintainer direction or using project spaces to promote unrelated commercial or
political campaigns may also be moderated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to project maintainers through the repository
hosting service or organization contact. Do not require a reporter to confront
the subject publicly. Maintainers should handle reports confidentially, avoid
conflicts of interest, gather relevant context, and choose proportionate action.

Actions may include a private warning, content removal, temporary participation
limits, removal from project spaces, or a permanent ban. Serious threats or
illegal conduct may be referred to the relevant platform or authorities. Good-
faith reporters and participants in an investigation must not be retaliated
against.

This code applies to project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions,
events, and public representation of the project.
